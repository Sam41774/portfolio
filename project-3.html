GoKart Frame Design

A lightweight, stiff frame design for a go-kart that balances performance, manufacturability, and safety.

ROLE Independent Design Project
TOOLS SolidWorks, CAD, Fabrication
TIMELINE Summer 2026
STATUS Ongoing
Overview Approach Challenges Results

Overview

This project focused on designing a compact go-kart frame with a strong emphasis on weight reduction and structural rigidity.

The goal was to create a frame that could be fabricated reliably while still feeling responsive and stable under load.

Approach

I approached the design from a structural-first perspective, iterating the geometry around stiffness and manufacturability.

  • Modeled the frame in SolidWorks and evaluated basic load paths.
  • Refined tube placement to reduce unnecessary weight without sacrificing rigidity.
  • Prepared a design that could be built using accessible fabrication methods.

Challenges

Balancing strength, packaging, and simplicity was the main challenge. The design needed to stay light while still being practical to build.

Results

The final frame concept was lightweight, structurally clean, and ready for further prototyping and testing.
