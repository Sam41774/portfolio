SolarCar Topshell Hinge

A lightweight, durable hinge design for the solar car's topshell, enabling smooth opening and closing while maintaining structural integrity.

ROLE Lead Designer
TOOLS SolidWorks, MATLAB
TIMELINE Spring 2026
STATUS Ongoing
Overview Approach Challenges Results

Overview

A lightweight hinge for the solar car topshell.

The original hinge concept was bulky and didn't fit well with the frame geometry.

Approach

Refined the geometry around strength, weight, and motion.

  • Reduced material where possible.
  • Improved the hinge sweep and mounting.
  • Improved ease of opening.

Challenges

Keeping it stiff without adding excessive weight.

Results

A cleaner, more manufacturable final hinge concept.
